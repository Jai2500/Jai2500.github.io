Hello 👋 !

My name is Jai.

I am a researcher in the [IMPACT Group](https://impact.ciirc.cvut.cz) at [CIIRC](https://www.ciirc.cvut.cz), Czech Technical University in Prague, advised by [Josef Sivic](https://people.ciirc.cvut.cz/~sivic/) and [Vladimir Petrik](https://petrikvladimir.github.io). I work on **world models for robotics**: 3D-consistent action-conditioned video models, world action models, RL post-training of world models, and using them to evaluate and improve robot policies. More broadly, I am interested in generative and self-supervised learning.

Recent projects:

- [**DepthWorld**](/depthworld/) (CoRL 2026): a 3D world model that jointly predicts multi-view RGB and metric depth, trained on DROID-3D, 71k DROID episodes we recalibrated to 4.9 mm median extrinsic error.
- [**PersistWorld**](/persistworld/) (ECCV 2026, 🏆 Outstanding Paper Award at UniWorld): RL post-training that keeps video world models stable over long autoregressive rollouts.
- [**CSCD**](https://cscd-skel.pages.dev) (WACV 2026): curve skeletonization of meshes and point clouds in the continuous domain, via discrete differential geometry.

Before CIIRC, I spent two years as a predoctoral research fellow in the Deep Learning and AI Lab at TCS Research with [Ramya Hebbalaguppe](https://rhebbalaguppe.github.io/), working on 3D shape analysis, confidence calibration in vision-language models, and evaluating and interpreting diffusion models.

I did my B.Tech. in Computer Science and M.S. by Research in Computational Natural Sciences at IIIT Hyderabad. My M.S. thesis with [Subhadip Mitra](https://sites.google.com/site/subhadipmitra/), ["Revisiting Deep Learning for Particle Physics"](https://ims-extr.iiit.ac.in/do.php?aAzDoo7cows3vOjCXFH1Q0E9+RBK3kaXx8sviQUimy8bvtFCofcoG10DFS3vlmQvFqP3eYOMHhrYUigKjvJFW/5pRDTN4Wdeucgqvp6bvj8Ctzp17Afjymi0/1qDBHKh0w5kbC6yvWc9Cu+Ly01sYA==), received the Ritesh Tiwari Outstanding MS Thesis Award. I still collaborate with Subhadip's group on machine learning for particle physics, such as self-supervised foundation models for collider data ([HEP-JEPA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.03933)).

I am always happy to chat about world models and robot learning, so feel free to reach out!
